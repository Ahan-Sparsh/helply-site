Helply

A communication aid for doctors and Deaf or hard-of-hearing patients who use Indian Sign Language. It works entirely on the iPhone, with no internet connection.

What it does

The doctor asks a question. The patient sees it on screen in English and Hindi and answers in Indian Sign Language, or by tapping an answer or typing. The doctor checks and approves every answer before it goes into the consultation summary.

Helply does not diagnose, give medical advice or keep a clinical record. Clinical judgement stays with the doctor.

Common questions

Does it need the internet?

No. Everything runs on the iPhone. The app is large (about 1.2 GB) because its models are built in, so download it over Wi-Fi.

Which iPhones does it run on?

iPhone XS / XR or later with iOS 18. An iPhone 13 or later is recommended.

The camera did not recognise a sign.

Make sure the patient's head, body down to the waist and both hands are in view, with the iPhone upright and good light in front of them. The patient can always answer by tapping or typing instead.

Is any patient information sent anywhere?

No. See the privacy policy.
