Privacy policy

Effective 12 October 2026

Helply collects no data. Everything it does happens on your iPhone. It has no servers, no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it sends nothing over the internet.

What Helply is

Helply is a communication aid. A doctor asks a question, a Deaf or hard-of-hearing patient answers in Indian Sign Language, by tapping or by typing, and the doctor approves each answer. It is not a medical device, does not diagnose, and is not a clinical record.

The camera

  • The patient is asked for consent, in English and Hindi, before the camera is used. They can say no and answer with buttons or by typing.
  • The camera is on only while the screen asks the patient to sign.
  • No video or photo is recorded or saved. The app reads the positions of points on the hands and body, uses them on the iPhone to recognise the sign, and then discards them.

Consultation content

  • Questions, answers and reports are processed on the iPhone by models built into the app. They are not sent anywhere.
  • A consultation is kept only while it is open. Starting a new one clears it.
  • At the end of a consultation the doctor may export a de-identified communication log. It leaves the iPhone only if the doctor shares it, using the iPhone's own share sheet, to a destination the doctor chooses. Helply does not receive it.

Hindi translation

Hindi translation uses Apple's on-device Translation. The first time it is used, iOS may offer to download Apple's Hindi language pack. That download is made by iOS and is covered by Apple's privacy policy; it can tell Apple which app asked and which languages, but never the text. Translation itself runs on the iPhone.

Children

Helply is intended for use by doctors. It collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
